# COMS472: Principles of Artificial Intelligence

Welcome to the COMS472 repository! This course covers foundational AI concepts following Russell & Norvig's *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th edition), spanning search algorithms, constraint satisfaction, and decision-making under uncertainty.

---

## 🛠 Core Topics

1. **Search Algorithms**  
   - Uninformed search (BFS, DFS, iterative deepening) and informed search (A*, greedy best-first).

2. **Local Search & Optimisation**  
   - Hill climbing, simulated annealing, and genetic algorithms for optimisation problems.

3. **Non-Deterministic Actions**  
   - AND-OR search trees and contingency planning for environments with uncertain action outcomes.

4. **Partial Observability**  
   - Belief-state search, sensorless problem formulations, and reasoning under incomplete information.

5. **Constraint Satisfaction Problems**  
   - Variable/value ordering heuristics, arc consistency, and backtracking search.

---

## 🔍 Assignments

- **Homework**: Problem sets reinforcing search algorithm implementation, CSP formulation, adversarial game-playing strategies, and planning under uncertainty.  
- **Notes**: Detailed study notes covering key lecture topics — search, local search, non-deterministic actions, sensorless problems, and partial percepts.

---

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

- Implement and compare uninformed and informed search strategies.  
- Formulate real-world problems as constraint satisfaction or planning tasks.  
- Design agents that reason and act under uncertainty and partial observability.  
- Evaluate trade-offs between optimality, completeness, and computational cost.
