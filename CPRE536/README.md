# CPRE536: Computer and Network Forensics

Welcome to the CPRE536 repository! This graduate-level course introduces the fundamentals of digital forensics — from forensic duplication and evidence analysis to incident response, cyber law, and preparing court-ready reports.

---

## 🛠 Core Topics

1. **Forensic Duplication & Analysis**  
   - Disk imaging, chain-of-custody procedures, and evidence integrity verification.

2. **Network Surveillance**  
   - Packet capture and traffic analysis for detecting intrusions and reconstructing attack timelines.

3. **Intrusion Detection & Response**  
   - IDS signatures, incident response workflows (identification, containment, eradication, recovery, lessons learned).

4. **Privacy & Anonymity**  
   - Anonymity techniques, pseudonymity, and privacy-preserving forensic methods.

5. **Cyber Law & Policy**  
   - Legal frameworks governing digital evidence, admissibility standards, and expert testimony.

---

## 🔍 Projects & Deliverables

- **Project 1**: Forensic analysis exercise applying disk imaging and evidence recovery techniques.  
- **Project 2**: Network forensics investigation involving traffic capture, intrusion detection, and timeline reconstruction.  
- **Project 3**: Comprehensive forensic case study integrating multiple analysis techniques.  
- **Term Paper & Presentation**: In-depth research paper on a selected digital forensics topic, with a final presentation to the class.

---

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

- Perform forensic duplication while maintaining chain-of-custody integrity.  
- Analyse network traffic to detect and document security incidents.  
- Follow structured incident response procedures.  
- Prepare professional forensic reports suitable for legal proceedings.  
- Reason about privacy, ethics, and legal constraints in digital investigations.
