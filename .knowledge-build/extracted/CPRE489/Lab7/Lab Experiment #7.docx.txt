Task 7
The capture shows the entire DORA process where D – Discover, O – Offer, R – Request, and A – Acknowledge. The DHCP Discover packet is used when we need to get a new IP address for our even PC and the packet is broadcasted to the entire network. The DHCP Offer packet is is sent back to the even PC assigning it the IP address of 10.0.50.4 as that was the first available address. Then the DHCP Request packet is sent from the even PC as a way to formally accept the offered IP address. Lastly, a DHCP Acknowledge packet is sent back to the even PC letting it know that 10.0.50.4 belongs to it.
