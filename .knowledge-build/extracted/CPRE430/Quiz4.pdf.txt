Purpose
This quiz is over the materials for Module 04. This quiz focuses on understanding network attack
point scenarios (including what network layers a bad actor can attack based on where they are
positioned in relation to the victim), defining the vulnerability types, and applying information about
both to classify and determine where an attack fits within the taxonomy of network-based
vulnerabilities.
