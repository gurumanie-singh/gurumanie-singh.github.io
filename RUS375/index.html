What I

RUS375 offers an interdisciplinary examination of Russian culture, history, and contemporary society. Through a series of module-based case studies, the course develops analytical writing, cross-cultural awareness, and the ability to evaluate complex geopolitical and societal issues from multiple perspectives.

Each module required a written case study applying course concepts to real-world scenarios, strengthening skills in research methodology, critical analysis, and persuasive writing — competencies that complement technical coursework in engineering and cybersecurity.
