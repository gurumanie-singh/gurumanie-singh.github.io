# RUS375: Topics in Russian Culture

Welcome to the RUS375 repository! This course offers an interdisciplinary examination of Russian culture, history, and contemporary society through module-based case study analyses.

---

## 🛠 Course Overview

An upper-level humanities course developing analytical writing, cross-cultural awareness, and critical thinking through the study of Russian cultural, social, and geopolitical themes. Each module culminates in a written case study applying course concepts to real-world scenarios.

---

## 🔍 Case Studies

- **Module 1 Case Study**: Foundational analysis of Russian cultural identity and historical context.  
- **Module 2 Case Study**: Socio-political dynamics and their influence on cultural expression and national identity.  
- **Module 4 Case Study**: Contemporary issues in Russian society, applying course frameworks to modern developments.  
- **Module 5 Case Study**: Geopolitical themes and cross-cultural perspectives.  
- **Module 7 Case Study**: Advanced synthesis of course concepts into cohesive analytical arguments.

---

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

- Analyse complex cultural and geopolitical issues from multiple perspectives.  
- Develop research methodology and persuasive academic writing skills.  
- Build cross-cultural communication competencies complementing technical coursework.  
- Evaluate historical and contemporary Russian society through interdisciplinary lenses.
